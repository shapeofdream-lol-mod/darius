# Darius balance pass - 2026-08-30

## Skill upgrades
- Q / W / R damage growth: 20% of the skill's base damage coefficient per additional Memory level.
- W slow-duration milestone: +0.08 s every 5 levels.
- E range milestone: +0.10 m every 5 levels; slow-duration milestone: +0.08 s every 5 levels.
- Base skill values are unchanged.

## Constellations
High-level slopes were compressed toward Shape of Dreams' native constellation progression while preserving level-1 identity. Gathering Storm and other intentional long-run mechanics remain scalable.

## New constellation: 诺克萨斯竞技场
- Destruction, 4 levels, required mastery 20.
- Active while at least one hostile is within 10 m. Enemy count is capped at 10.
- Base bonuses by level: AD 5/6/7.5/9%, AS 8/10/12/15%, MS 3/4/5/6%, Armor 10/12.5/15/18%.
- Every missing enemy below 10 increases the whole package by 15%; at one enemy the scarcity multiplier is x2.35.
- Elite nearby: x1.6 total rank multiplier.
- Boss nearby: x2.8 total rank multiplier and overrides Elite.
- Single Boss: x6.58 total multiplier. At level 4 this is about +59.2% AD, +98.7% AS, +39.5% MS and +118.4% Armor.
