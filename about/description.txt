简体中文

诺克萨斯之手 · 德莱厄斯

将德莱厄斯作为独立旅行者加入《梦之形》。被动【出血 / 诺克萨斯之力】为角色内置机制；Q【大杀四方】与 R【诺克萨斯断头台】开局自带，W【致残打击】与 E【无情铁手】通过灵活星座获得，并提供【经典 / 神王 / 灌篮高手 / 机神】四套皮肤。每套皮肤均绑定独立 GLB 模型档案、骨骼与动画映射，不假设模型分块或动画数量相同；皮肤不改变任何玩法和数值。专属星座已扩展到 38 个，围绕【出血】、追猎、【诺克萨斯之力】、处决与角色背景提供多方向强化。

🔊 音效设置
Mod 设置中提供 Q / W / E / R / 普攻 / 闪避 / 语音 7 条独立音量滑条（0%～200%，默认100%），修改后立即作用于该旅行者 Mod 自己播放的对应音效并保存设置。

⚠ 兼容性说明
部分精粹、多人模式或后续游戏更新可能出现兼容问题，建议重要存档提前备份。遇到问题欢迎在创意工坊留言，并尽量附上复现方式与日志。

English
Darius joins Shape of Dreams as an independent Traveler. Q and R are available from the start, while W and E are granted by separate flexible constellations. Bleed, Noxian Might, execution, four matched skins, constellation upgrades, and per-skill audio controls are included.

日本語

English

Darius · The Hand of Noxus

Adds Darius to Shape of Dreams as an independent Traveler. Hemorrhage / Noxian Might is built into the Traveler; Q - Decimate and R - Noxian Guillotine are starting Memories, while W - Crippling Strike and E - Apprehend are obtained through Flexible Constellation stars. Classic, God-King, Dunkmaster, and Mecha skins are included. Each outfit has its own GLB structure, rig, and animation profile rather than sharing fixed model assumptions, without changing gameplay or balance. The dedicated Constellation contains 38 stars built around Hemorrhage, pursuit, Noxian Might, executions, and Darius-themed progression.

🔊 SFX Settings
Mod settings provide seven independent Q / W / E / R / Basic Attack / Dodge / Voice volume sliders (0-200%, default 100%). Changes apply to this Traveler mod's corresponding SFX immediately and are persisted.

⚠ Compatibility
Some Essences, multiplayer interactions, or future game updates may cause compatibility issues. Back up important saves, and please include reproduction steps and logs when reporting bugs.

⚠ Multiplayer and Mid-run Exit Warning
Multiplayer has not been fully validated, so I cannot predict every issue that may occur. This does not mean the mod cannot be used in multiplayer. If you encounter a multiplayer problem, feedback is welcome; please include reproduction steps whenever possible.
Please avoid closing the game in the middle of a run. Doing so may cause the next launch to become stuck on the experience settlement screen in the main menu. This happens because main-menu experience settlement runs before mods have begun loading, so modded character data cannot be resolved at that moment. If this occurs, simply close and launch the game again.

🤖 AI-assisted Development
OpenAI GPT / Codex was used extensively for coding, log analysis, and debugging. Character design, gameplay decisions, balance direction, and in-game testing were handled by the author. AI was used as a development tool, not as a one-click generator for the entire mod.
